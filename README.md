## Waheed Adeagbo

Computational physicist (Dr. rer. nat.) moving into data science. I spent about fifteen years simulating materials
at German universities and now work with Python, machine learning and locally hosted language models. Based in Halle (Saale).

*Physiker und Data Scientist aus Halle (Saale). Ich arbeite auf Deutsch und Englisch.*

### Projects worth a look

- **[verwaltungs-rag](https://github.com/wadeagbo/verwaltungs-rag)**: a local RAG prototype that answers questions about
  Baden-Württemberg administrative law, cites its sources and says when the documents have no answer. Runs fully offline
  with Ollama and Qdrant, and comes with its own evaluation.
- **[Machine_Learning_Projects](https://github.com/wadeagbo/Machine_Learning_Projects)**: forecasting CO levels from air
  quality data, classifying wind turbine performance, denoising hyperspectral images.
- **[c1-grammatik](https://github.com/wadeagbo/c1-grammatik)**: German grammar at C1 level in ten volumes of LaTeX slides
  and handouts, plus a workbook and Anki cards.
- **[SPICE_Academy](https://github.com/wadeagbo/SPICE_Academy)**: data science bootcamp work, including heart disease and
  diabetes prediction.
- **[DCI_projects_django](https://github.com/wadeagbo/DCI_projects_django)**: Django apps and REST APIs from my backend
  developer training.
